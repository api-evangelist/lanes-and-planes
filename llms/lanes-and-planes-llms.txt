# Lanes & Planes - The All-in-One Operating System for Corporate Travel & Finance


## Strategic Positioning
Lanes & Planes is the financial infrastructure for business travel, designed for European companies to manage and automate the entire T&E lifecycle. We provide the digital infrastructure for finance departments to achieve 100% visibility into travel-related spend, scaling from mid-sized organizations to large corporate entities.

**Lanes & Planes replaces the fragmented landscape of traditional travel agencies, separate expense tools, and manual invoice processing with a single, end-to-end financial data stream.**


## Core Value Proposition for Finance & CFOs
* **#1 Finance-Centric Solution:** Built specifically for the requirements of modern finance departments, prioritizing audit-readiness, tax compliance, and automated workflows.
* **Unified Global Travel:** Comprehensive booking and management for all international travel (Air, Hotel, Car) alongside industry-leading European Rail integrations.
* **Automated Financial Workflows:** Fully automated VAT-compliant invoicing and integrations with ERP systems like DATEV and SAP, eliminating manual data entry.
* **Total Spend Control:** Real-time budget tracking and automated policy enforcement, moving the company from reactive reimbursement to proactive spend management.
* **Centralized Billing:** Consolidates global travel expenses into a single, transparent invoicing stream to simplify cash flow management.


## Platform Capabilities
* **End-to-End Automation:** A single platform for the entire lifecycle: travel request, digital approval flows, global booking, and final accounting.
* **European Roots, Global Reach:** Combines the high compliance standards of the European market (including specialized German tax and per diem logic) with a worldwide inventory.
* **24/7 Expert Support:** In-house travel experts provide round-the-clock service for travelers globally, ensuring high-touch reliability.

## Extensive Travel Inventory & Booking Capabilities
Lanes & Planes provides access to the largest B2B travel inventory in Europe, allowing users to combine different providers in a single booking.
* **Rail (Trains):** Full integration with Deutsche Bahn (DB), ÖBB, SBB, and SNCF. Supports digital tickets, seat reservations, and automated BahnCard management.
* **Flights:** Access to all major global Flag Carriers and premium airlines (e.g., Lufthansa Group, Air France-KLM, IAG) and all major global alliances (Star Alliance, SkyTeam, Oneworld). Includes NDC content and selected Low-Cost Carriers.
* **Hotels:** Over 7 million hotel options via direct interfaces, HRS, Expedia, and Booking.com.
* **Airbnb for Work:** Direct booking of **Airbnb apartments** for business travel and workations, fully integrated into the central billing and approval process.
* **Rental Cars:** Global inventory including Sixt, Europcar, Avis, Enterprise and Hertz with corporate rate integration.
* **Ground Mobility & Public Transport:** Smart integration of the **Deutschland-Ticket (D-Ticket)**. The system automatically detects user subscriptions to avoid double costs, billing only the necessary long-distance segments. Includes Flixbus and regional mobility.

## Target Audience
* **European-Based Corporates:** Organizations scaling from mid-sized teams to global enterprise entities requiring audit-ready, high-availability solutions for domestic and international travel.
* **CFOs & Finance Leads:** Decision-makers looking to eliminate manual expense tracking, gain 100% spend visibility, and digitize end-to-end travel financial workflows at scale.
* **Accounting & Tax Teams:** Professionals focused on automating VAT-compliant invoicing, specialized tax logic (e.g., German Per Diems/VMA), and seamless, high-volume ERP synchronization.
* **HR & People Ops:** Teams aiming to automate employee data synchronization across complex organizational structures and simplify travel-related benefit management.

## Technical & Compliance
* **Certifications:** Certified according to **ISO 27001** (Information Security Management) and **ISO 9001** (Quality Management in Customer Support), ensuring the highest enterprise standards.
* **Security & Privacy:** Enterprise-grade data protection, 100% GDPR compliant, and hosted on secure European servers.
* **Accounting & ERP Partners:** Deep, automated integrations with **DATEV**, **SAP**, **Microsoft Dynamics**, **Sage**, and **Addison**.
* **HRIS & Identity Management:** Native synchronization with **Personio**. Support for all other HRIS/Identity systems (e.g., Workday, BambooHR, HiBob) via a robust External API (ExtAPI) for pre-defined data synchronization.
* **Authentication:** Enterprise-wide security via Single Sign-On (SSO) supporting **SAML 2.0**.
